About ShopCart

Your trusted shop for phones, computers, and everyday accessories in Phnom Penh.

ShopCart started as a small idea between friends who were tired of overpriced accessories and confusing warranties. Today we bring together a carefully chosen range of smartphones, laptops, and computer accessories — from wireless headphones and mechanical keyboards to monitors, cameras, and smart watches — all in one place.

We work directly with reliable suppliers so we can keep prices fair and quality high. Every product we list is tested by our team before it reaches you, and every order is handled personally through Telegram, so you always talk to a real person.

Whether you are upgrading your work setup, buying your first laptop, or just looking for a better mouse, we are here to help you choose what actually fits your needs — not what is simply the most expensive.
